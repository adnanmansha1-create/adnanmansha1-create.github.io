04 / LEADERSHIP APPROACH

Calm in style.
Clear in judgement.

My leadership approach combines calm judgement with clear accountability: understand the business, state a position, and make sure the team can deliver.

01

Start with the decision.

Lead with the issue and the recommendation. Connect the numbers to the options, trade-offs and action leadership needs to take.

02

Keep cash in view.

Read performance alongside liquidity, working capital and funding needs. A credible plan needs a credible cash path.

03

Lead through people and ownership.

Set clear responsibilities and priorities, challenge constructively and support delivery. Make improvements part of how the team works, with technology serving the business need.

ENTERING A NEW MANDATE

My first 90 days:
understand, align, deliver.

A starting agenda to agree with the CEO and leadership team, adapted to the business and its priorities.

DAYS 1–30 / UNDERSTAND

Establish the business priorities.

Understand the value drivers, liquidity position, principal risks and expectations of owners and leadership. Assess the team’s capacity and the information needed for critical decisions.

Outcome: An agreed view of the highest-priority issues and immediate actions.

DAYS 31–60 / ALIGN

Agree the financial response.

Challenge the plan against cash capacity and downside scenarios. Agree performance priorities, decision-focused reporting and responsibilities with business leaders.

Outcome: A prioritised finance agenda with accountable owners and measures of success.

DAYS 61–90 / DELIVER

Set execution in motion.

Advance the agreed priorities and review early progress. Present a sequenced 12-month finance roadmap covering people, performance, governance and systems, with costs and benefits made explicit.

Outcome: A practical delivery roadmap and an agreed review rhythm.
